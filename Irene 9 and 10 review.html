Irene's English Class Review

Daily Conversation, Travel & "What Were You Like 10 Years Ago?"

1. Greetings & Daily Conversation

  • How is everything so far this week?
    → Everything is good so far this week.
  • Do you have any plans for the weekend?
    → No, I don't have any plans for the weekend.
  • Job titles: nail technician / manicurist (手部美甲師) / pedicurist (足部美甲師)
  • Time phrase: these days = nowadays (最近、現今)

2. Grammar Focus

A. Near vs. Far & Singular vs. Plural

Near (近)Far (遠)
Placehere (這裡)there (那裡)
Singular (單數)this (這個)that (那個) — That person is immature.
Plural (複數)these (這些)those (那些) — Those people are immature.

B. Past Tense (過去式) & "Used to"

  • is → was: It is very delicious. → It was very delicious. / How was the hike?
  • eat → ate: I ate some bento at Fenchihu (奮起湖).
  • see → saw: I saw many ancient sacred trees.
  • hike → hiked: We hiked for 2 hours.
  • used to + V (過去常常): I used to eat birthday cake and blow out candles when I was a child.

C. Talking About Life Changes (Engoo Lesson 99)

  • Past description: What were you like 10 years ago? → I was extremely introverted.
  • Present Perfect (現在完成式 have/has + p.p.):
    How have you changed? → I've become more sociable and confident.
    How has your life changed? → I got married, bought a house, and got a dog.
  • Future & Possibility: What do you think your life will be like in 10 years?
    I might be wrinkled and gray! (might 可能)

3. Word Forms: Noun vs. Adjective

  • extrovert (n.) / extroverted (adj.) 外向的
    I'm an extrovert. = I'm extroverted.
  • introvert (n.) / introverted (adj.) 內向的
    I'm an introvert. = I'm introverted.
  • society (n.) 社會 / social, sociable (adj.) 善於交際的
  • activity (n.) 活動 / active (adj.) 活躍的 (physical activity)
  • energy (n.) 能量 / energetic (adj.) 精力充沛的
  • electricity (n.) 電 / electric (adj.) 電動的 / electronic (adj.) 電子的
  • youth (n.) 青春 / youthful (adj.) 年輕有活力的
  • wrinkle (n.) 皺紋 / wrinkled (adj.) 有皺紋的

4. "-able" Suffixes (可以...的) & Key Vocabulary

  • affordable 付得起的、平價的
  • collectable 可收藏的 / 收藏品 (n.)
  • enjoyable 令人愉快的
  • laughable 可笑的
  • manageable 可應付的、撐得住的
    The workload this week is manageable.
  • readable 易讀的、清晰的
  • reasonable 合理的 (The price is reasonable.)
  • usable 可使用的、沒壞的 (This pencil is usable.)
  • ancient (adj.) 古老的
  • sacred (adj.) 神聖的
  • sharp (adj.) 銳利的；聰明的
  • mature / immature 成熟的 / 不成熟的
  • outgoing (adj.) 外向的
  • fetch (v.) 接住；去拿來
  • old and gray 年老髮白
  • Engoo life events: retire (退休), graduate (畢業), move out of home (搬出家裡), get married (結婚)
